Bass Pedal Trainer
Keyboard only

1 Connect the pad

Plug the kick pad into an instrument (Hi-Z) or line input of your audio interface with a mono 6.3 mm jack cable. Start with the input gain low.

No pad connected? Space, F or J work as a stand-in pad everywhere in the app.

Tuning tips
  • Double triggers (one stroke counts twice): raise Ringing suppression (a longer decay), then Retrigger mask. If soft strokes right after loud ones are lost, lower the suppression.
  • Missed soft strokes: lower Threshold or raise Gain.
  • Triggers when resting the foot: run the noise check. It sets the threshold above the resting noise.
  • Keep the mask below the gap between your fastest strokes: 16ths at 220 BPM are 68 ms apart, 32nds are 34 ms apart.

3 Calibrate latency

Audio interfaces and browsers add delay. Play one stroke with each click (4 count-in clicks + 16 clicks at 90 BPM). The app measures your average offset and removes it from every score.

Sounds

The volume follows how hard you hit. Pad strokes play straight from the audio engine, which gives the lowest delay. Use headphones if the speakers are loud.
